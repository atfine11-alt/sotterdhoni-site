Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

  1. What we access. When the owner of the Sotter Dhoni account authorizes the tool through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives basic profile information (user.info.basic: display name and avatar) and an access token for the owner's own account.
  2. Why. We use this access only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok drafts (video.upload scope).
  3. What we do not do. We do not access, collect, sell or share data of any other TikTok user. We do not run ads. We do not share the data with third parties.
  4. Storage. The access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a private file. No server or database holds it.
  5. Retention and deletion. The token is deleted when the owner revokes access in TikTok settings or deletes the file. To ask for any data deletion, contact us.
  6. Children. The tool is not directed to children and has no other users.
  7. Contact. sotterdhoni@gmail.com
